About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in 1979. It provided training facilities for students and research facilities for lecturers.

In June 1996, the institution was renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It later became Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, it was officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT).

Today, UMT offers various academic programmes in marine science, fisheries, technology, engineering, maritime studies, business and information technology. The university is committed to producing knowledgeable graduates and promoting environmental sustainability.
